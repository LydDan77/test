Questions

questions, answered

Is this a sales pitch?

No. It's an education session led by a licensed provider, with live questions. Pricing is only discussed at a private consultation, never on the webinar.

Who is a candidate?

Not everyone. Candidacy depends on your joints, your history and a provider's judgment. A consultation is how we find out, and the webinar is where you learn enough to decide whether you want one.

Do I have to buy anything to enter the giveaway?

No. Registering is one entry, watching live earns three more, and each friend who registers through your link adds one. Entries are never tied to booking or buying. Official rules at [rules link].

What if I can't watch live?

Register anyway. Every registrant gets the replay the next morning. Live attendance is what earns the bonus entries, so join if you can.

How does the holiday pricing work?

Complete a consultation by December 31 and, if the provider confirms you're a candidate, schedule treatment by January 31. The $1,000 single-area and $2,500 multi-area pricing applies to treatment scheduled in that window.

Is financing available?

Yes, through Cherry. You can pre-qualify in about 60 seconds with no impact to your credit. Approval and terms are subject to Cherry's review.

Can I bring someone?

Please do. A spouse, a parent, a friend, or a whole living room. Watch party hosts get a kit, and everyone who registers through your link adds an entry to your name.
